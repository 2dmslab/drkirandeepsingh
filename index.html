Research Interests

What we explore

Our group spans from fundamental quantum transport in atomically thin crystals to spin-orbit phenomena and applied device engineering — bridging the gap between 2D nanoscale physics, spintronics, and real-world technology.

⬡ 01

2D Semiconductors & Devices

Synthesis and device engineering of TMDs — MoS₂, WS₂, WSe₂ — for field-effect transistors, photodetectors, and flexible electronics. We grow monolayers by CVD and probe their transport down to few-atomic layers.

CVD GrowthFETsPhotodetectors
🧲 02

Magnetism & Spintronics

Exploring room-temperature magnetic order in van der Waals layered materials. We investigate spin transport, magnetoresistance, and anomalous Hall effects in ultrathin magnetic films and heterostructures.

vdW MagnetsHall EffectThin Films
⚡ 03

Thin Film Supercapacitors

Developing 2D material electrode architectures for high-performance supercapacitors. We use electrochemical impedance spectroscopy and cyclic voltammetry to optimise energy density and cycle stability.

Energy StorageMXenesEIS / CV
🔬 04

Solid-State Hydrogen Storage

Engineering metal hydride systems and nanostructured materials for reversible solid-state hydrogen storage, focusing on gravimetric density, kinetics, and thermodynamic stability.

Metal HydridesNanostructuringClean Energy
✦ 05

Multifunctional Nanomaterials

Synthesis and characterisation of phase-change materials for memory and multiferroics exhibiting coupled magnetic and ferroelectric order — targeting non-volatile memory and sensor technologies.

Phase-ChangeMultiferroicsMemory
